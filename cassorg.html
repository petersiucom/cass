Welcome to CASS

We provide a comprehensive range of social and welfare services for every stage of life, including home ageing, residential aged care, disability, vocation & training, settlement & health, child care, and many more. Our services cover a wide geographical area including Metropolitan Sydney, Wollongong, and now even getting a standing in Victoria!

Explore Our Services How Can We Help?

Comprehensive Community Services

CASS Care provides a wide range of community services and activities for people residing in Metropolitan Sydney and Wollongong.

🏥

Residential Aged Care

Compassionate 24-hour care in modern facilities designed to support independence and dignity for seniors.

🏡

Home Ageing

Personalized in-home support services helping seniors maintain their independence and quality of life.

♿

Disability Services

Specialized support and care programs for individuals with disabilities and their families.

🎓

Vocation and Training

Professional development and training programs to empower individuals and build community capacity.

🌱

Settlement and Health

Culturally sensitive health services and settlement support for new migrants and refugees.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

Child Care

Quality early childhood education and care services in multicultural community environments.

🏘️

Elderly Hostel

Community-focused residential care with a warm, family-like atmosphere for seniors.

🔗

Other Services

Specialized programs including Care Finder Services and Aged Care Connect Assistance.
